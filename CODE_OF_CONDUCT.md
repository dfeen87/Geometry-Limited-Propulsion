# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, trolling, or insulting/derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers listed in the README.
